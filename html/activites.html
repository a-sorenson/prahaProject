My Top Activities

Hockey Game

I always forget how much I love going to sports games so when I was able to go see the Prague hockey team play it was genuinely such a fun time. Though our group had more fun trying to build the largest cup snake. They might complain about Americans being loud, but we got on the jumbo tron multiple times so we are no doubt entertaining. Much like Minnesota they do not joke about their Hockey.

Cooking Class

I loved any of the hands on activities that my program did in Prague and this Czech cooking class was so fun. The chef was so funny and walked us through really good recipies. On top of that, I won the egg white beating competition so my baking skills came in clutch. I plan to do more cooking classes when I travel.

Brno Trip

On the second week of the program they did an overnight trip to Brno. It felt like I was back on those really cool field trips that were overnight to a camp. The entire town has such a different vibe than Prague, and we ended up having a great time. We visited a WW2 bunker, as ossuary, and just explored around town. This was also the push that helped me book a few more trips to other Czech towns.
