Cultural Education

Many of the activities that my program scheduled abroad focused on hands on interaction with the history of the Czech Republic. We participated in Christmas traditions, cheered on the local hockey team, and attended an orchestral concert at the National Theater.

Unique Experiences

There is a handful of activities that I never expected to be part of my study abroad experience. I played real life mario kart, held a bald eagle, and helped create a vase from molten glass. All of these were tied closely with Czech culture and past times but had their own special element that made them stand out.
